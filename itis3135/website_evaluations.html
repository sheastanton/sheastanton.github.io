Website Evaluations

This page evaluates two websites using the CRAP design principles: contrast, repetition, alignment, and proximity.

Nike - Strong Design Example

  • Contrast: Nike uses strong contrast between bold images, large text, and simple background colors. This helps products and promotions stand out clearly.
  • Repetition: The site repeats similar fonts, button styles, product cards, and spacing throughout the pages, making the design feel consistent.
  • Alignment: Text, images, navigation links, and product sections are neatly aligned, which makes the website easy to scan and use.
  • Proximity: Related items are grouped well, such as product images, names, prices, and shopping buttons being placed close together.

Ling's Cars Museum - Weak Design Example

  • Contrast: The site uses many bright colors, patterns, and animated elements at once. Instead of helping important content stand out, the contrast becomes overwhelming and distracting.
  • Repetition: Some visual styles repeat, but the repetition feels chaotic because there are too many competing fonts, colors, images, and decorative elements.
  • Alignment: The layout feels crowded and uneven. Many elements compete for attention, which makes it harder for users to know where to look first.
  • Proximity: Related information is not always clearly grouped. The page has so much content close together that it can be difficult to quickly separate sections or understand the main purpose.
