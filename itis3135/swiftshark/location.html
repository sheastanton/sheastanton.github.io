Swift Shark Location

Where to Find Us

Swift Shark is imagined as a local custom garage located near busy roads, student areas, and weekend car meet locations. The shop is designed to feel clean and modern instead of crowded or confusing, giving customers a clear place to discuss their build ideas.

Customers can visit the garage for consultations, part planning, and scheduled modification appointments. The location includes a work bay, a small waiting area, and a photo spot where finished cars can be shown after the final detail.

Shop Environment

The garage is set up to support small and medium modification jobs such as lighting upgrades, tint planning, wrap accents, wheel consultations, and detail work. The space is organized so customers can see examples of colors, materials, and build styles before choosing a direction.

Swift Shark also uses the space to meet with customers before ordering parts. This helps prevent wasted money and makes sure every upgrade fits the car correctly before the installation begins.
