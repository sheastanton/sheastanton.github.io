Swift Shark Appointments

Hours and Appointments

Swift Shark works mainly by appointment so each car gets enough time and attention. Some upgrades can be completed the same day, while larger changes like wraps, suspension work, or full appearance packages may need more time.

The shop also offers weekend consultation blocks for customers who want to plan their build before buying parts. This keeps the process organized and helps avoid wasted money on parts that do not fit the car correctly.

  • Monday to Friday: 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  • Saturday: 11:00 AM - 4:00 PM
  • Sunday: Closed
  • Build consultations are by appointment only.
