Swift Shark Process

How the Build Process Works

Swift Shark starts every build with a consultation. The customer explains the car, the budget, the style they want, and whether the vehicle is used as a daily driver, weekend car, or show-style project. This helps the shop understand what upgrades make sense.

After the consultation, Swift Shark creates a simple build plan. The plan may include appearance upgrades, wheel fitment, lighting changes, wrap ideas, detail work, or interior improvements. Once the customer approves the plan, the modifications are scheduled in stages.

Build Steps

The process is organized so customers know what is happening before any work begins. This keeps the project realistic and helps customers avoid buying parts that do not match their vehicle or their goals.

Each finished build ends with a final check and detail. Swift Shark reviews the upgrades, confirms the customer is satisfied, and photographs the finished vehicle so the owner can see the complete transformation.
