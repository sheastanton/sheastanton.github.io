Swift Shark Study Guide Documentation

This documentation explains how to use the Swift Shark study system. The goal is to help students organize assignments, prepare for exams, and manage study time more effectively.

Getting Started

Begin by listing every class, assignment, exam, and deadline in one place. This gives you a clear view of the full academic ocean before you start swimming.

Step 1: List all deadlines.

Weekly Planning

Each week, divide your work into small tasks. A Swift Shark does not wait until the wave crashes; it moves early and stays ahead.

Step 2: Break large assignments into smaller tasks.

Focused Study Blocks

Study blocks should be short, focused, and realistic. A strong study session is better than a long distracted one.

Step 3: Study for 25 to 45 minutes at a time.

Exam Preparation

Before an exam, review notes, make flashcards, and practice explaining the material in your own words.

Step 4: Practice before reviewing passively.

Final Review

The final review should focus on weak areas. Do not only study what already feels easy.

Step 5: Attack your weakest topics first.
